## Non-claims

- `ScriptedProvider` proves provider parameterization, not a second
  production Worker runtime; the POSIX supervisor + reference worker
  remains the approved runtime. No running-Invocation migration and no
  `resume` claim (`resume: unsupported` for v0.1).
- Skipped rows (WC-START-02, WC-LIVE-01, WC-STOP-02 on `PosixProvider`)
  are reported by the runner, not substituted — a real process cannot
  model a lost identity capture or an uninspectable-liveness read, and
  the POSIX port keeps no signal audit log.
